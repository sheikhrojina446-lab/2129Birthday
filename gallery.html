✦ THREE TYPES OF LOVE ✦

Love Is Of Three Types

NASEEB
FIRST
When you meet someone and they accept you the way you are,That is called - NASEEB (Luck).
MUKADDAR
SECOND
When you wish for someone and that person becomes yours,That is called - MUKADDAR (Destiny).
ISHQ
THIRD
When you love someone who never becomes yours in your whole life,That is called - ISHQ (True Love).
As the saying goes -
What you get for free is called Luck, what you get after praying for is called Destiny,
But the one who never becomes yours yet stays alive in your heart, God is witness, that is called True Love (Ishq). ❤️
